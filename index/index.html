The Finding

The loudest brands aren't the fastest-moving ones.

The two brands making the most noise — HYROX and WHOOP — have flat search demand. They're already at a high plateau. The real acceleration is Oura, Third Space and Planet Fitness. A vibes list ranks the loudest; a scored one shows where attention is still growing.

The writeup judges.

Each brand earns 0–10 on six public signals, weighted to a 100-point score. A brand growing on discounts gets called out for it. Nothing here is a vibe.

Sources: company filings and IR, press releases, and Google Trends (demand trajectory, quarter over quarter). Consumer brands only — no B2B software.
